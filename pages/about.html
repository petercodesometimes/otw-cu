A focused first version

This MVP plans your stops around a single day’s classes, task durations, deadlines, sample opening hours, estimated walks, and an arrival buffer. It evaluates every feasible order for up to eight selected stops.

Locations and service hours are illustrative, unverified data—not live university information. Check official service hours and eligibility before a trip. Walking estimates use straight-line coordinates with a detour factor, not a directions API.

Next development milestones: verified service data, real walking routes, recurring schedules, accounts, and shared lists. Walking buddies and friend matching are future features and are not active in this prototype.
